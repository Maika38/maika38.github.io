01Crown HoursShift & pay tracker

My shifts always change. I didn't even know if I got the right pay.

The pain
Hospitality rosters change all the time, so the hours I actually work rarely match the original roster. I had no easy way to check my pay was right.
What I built
A shift tracker built with Claude. An 11 PM reminder nudges me, I log the shift in seconds (normal, sick, holiday or day off), and it works out paid hours after breaks, flags overtime and builds a timecard check for every fortnight.
The outcome
When my payslip arrives, I compare it with my time log, spot any day that's missing pay and chase it up. No more hours quietly disappearing.
Prototype in Figma
I connected Claude to Figma to map the whole app as a clickable prototype flow, screen by screen.
